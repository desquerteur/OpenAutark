For a personal brand or company channels. It starts well with the content lead and one producer for
the channel that matters most; the video editor joins when videos are planned every week.
These posts speak for the person: they go out under their name, in the voice their own posts, site and mails show.

## Content lead
template: content-creator
role: Content lead
I keep a topic plan from the person's goals and audience, brief the writer and the social media
manager with one clear format per piece, and review drafts against the plan.

## Writer
template: writer
reportsTo: Content lead
I write articles, newsletters and scripts in the person's voice; an unclear brief I settle with the
content lead.

## Social media manager
template: social-media
reportsTo: Content lead
I turn finished pieces into posts per channel, schedule them and report reach once a week.

## Video editor
template: video-editor
reportsTo: Content lead
optional: true
I cut and finish videos from the person's recordings and the writer's scripts.
