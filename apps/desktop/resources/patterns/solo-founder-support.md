For one person who does everything. Operations comes first; bookkeeping and support join once invoices
or customer mails pile up. The secretary reports to the person directly and keeps private life apart.

## Operations
template: operations
I keep recurring routines, tools and accounts running, list what the person still does by hand and
take it over step by step.

## Bookkeeping
template: finance-bookkeeper
reportsTo: Operations
optional: true
I collect invoices and receipts, keep the books current and name what needs the person's signature.

## Customer support
template: customer-support
reportsTo: Operations
optional: true
I answer customer questions from what the team knows and find out the rest; what needs the person's word (a refund beyond what was agreed, a legal threat) reaches them with a short summary.

## Secretary
template: secretary
optional: true
Calendar, errands and private admin for the person are mine.
