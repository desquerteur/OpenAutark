# Social posting

## 1. Prepare
- Goal of the post (reach, click, reply, announcement) and the account.
- I write in the brand voice (`writing-style`) and adapt it per platform, since each has its own
  reader.
- Media in the right format (when unsure, I check the platform's current specs):

| Platform | Typical format | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram feed | 1080×1350 (4:5) or 1080×1080 | first line is the hook; hashtags few and specific |
| Reels / TikTok / Shorts | 1080×1920 (9:16), short | captions burned in, safe zones top/bottom |
| LinkedIn | text first, image 1200×627 or document | no link in the first lines if the human prefers reach; plain professional tone |
| X / Threads | short text, image 16:9 | one idea per post; threads for more |
| Facebook | image or video, link posts | groups have own rules |

- Accessibility: alt text for images, captions for videos.
- Facts and claims are true and sourced: an invented testimonial or number costs more than it brings.

## 2. Sensitive topics
- Politics, legal, crisis or personal life: I show the person the exact text and media first:

## 3. Publish or schedule
- I use the platform's own scheduler or the connected tool; otherwise I create a schedule for
  myself at the posting time.
- Good times come from the account's own analytics; without data, I post on weekday mornings
  in the audience's time zone and learn from results.
- After posting I open the post, check text, media, links, tags, and record the URL.

## 4. Content log
I append to the social log in the task workspace: date, platform, account, link, topic, format,
first-week results. It keeps me from repeats and feeds reports.

## 5. Replies routine
I schedule replies after ~1 h and ~24 h (longer for video platforms, see
`proactive-follow-ups`). I answer questions, thank genuine comments briefly, let trolls be and hide
spam. Complaints, legal, press and partnership requests go with a short summary to whoever speaks for the company there.

## What keeps an account healthy
- Lasting reach comes from good posts and real replies. Bought followers, engagement pods,
  mass-following and automation the platform forbids get accounts limited, so I pace actions like a
  person would.
- A DM goes to someone it is clearly relevant for; the same message to many reads as spam.
- Ads and sponsorships are disclosed.
- Private information about anyone stays out of posts.
