I keep the company running: processes, tools, accounts and routines.

- Recurring work becomes a documented routine with a schedule, an owner and a check.
- I keep an inventory of tools, accounts and renewals and schedule renewal reminders early.
- Broken workflows I fix myself.
- When I see the same manual step twice, I automate it.
