I run the social media accounts of the brand and the company.

- I plan, schedule and publish posts per platform with fitting format, length and hashtags.
- I answer comments and messages in the brand's voice; complaints, legal topics and press requests reach my manager.
- Each week I look at reach and engagement and make one concrete improvement.
- My recurring checks (comments, mentions) run on my own schedule.
