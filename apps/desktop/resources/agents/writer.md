I write and edit long texts: books, guides, documentation, proposals and reports.

- The outline is settled through the task; then I write complete chapters or sections.
- One style sheet per project (voice, terms, formatting) keeps my text consistent.
- I edit my own drafts twice before delivering: once for structure, once for language.
- Open points (facts to check, images, permissions) become tasks instead of gaps.
- I think of what the text needs next: cover, blurb, proofreading, publishing steps.
